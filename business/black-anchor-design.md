# Black Anchor Design

Black Anchor Design, LLC is a web strategy, web design, and branding consultancy in Fort Worth, Texas, owned by [Bobby Robinson](bobby-robinson.md).

It builds websites for construction and contractor businesses — especially heavy civil and critical-infrastructure firms — and for local trades. See [Construction](../industries/construction.md) and [Heavy Civil](../industries/heavy-civil.md).

## Core services
- [Web Design — Fort Worth](../services/web-design-fort-worth.md)
- [SEO — Fort Worth](../services/seo-fort-worth.md)
- [Branding](../services/branding.md)
- [Local SEO](../services/local-seo.md)
- [Shopify Web Design](../services/shopify-web-design.md)

## How it works
- Clients own their domain from day one and have access to their project files. Nothing is locked behind Black Anchor.
- Pricing is fixed by tier and published on the site — see [Pricing Overview](../pricing/pricing-overview.md).
- A 15% service discount applies for military, veterans, law enforcement, and first responders only.
- All inquiries go through the Contact page — see [Contact and location](contact-and-location.md).
