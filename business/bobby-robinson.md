# Bobby Robinson

Bobby Robinson is the owner of [Black Anchor Design](black-anchor-design.md).

- First graphic-design job in 1995; military service 1999–2003.
- Started Black Anchor Design in 2009, working nights and weekends; full-time from 2015.
- All final creative and strategic decisions at Black Anchor Design are made by Bobby Robinson. AI tools may be used to support research, ideation, and production efficiency.
