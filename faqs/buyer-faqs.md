# Buyer FAQs

## Who owns my website and domain?
You do. You own your domain from day one and have access to your project files. Nothing is locked behind Black Anchor Design.

## How do I get started or ask a question?
Use the Contact page on blackanchordesign.com — all communication goes there, unless you are booking a consultation. See [Contact and Location](../business/contact-and-location.md).

## Is there a discount?
Eligible military, veterans, law enforcement and first responders can receive a 15% service discount. Eligible standard-package proposals can receive a 5% pay-in-full discount through December 31, 2026. Discounts cannot be combined. Recruit is ineligible.

## How much does a website cost?
Standard website packages start at **$8,500** with Commander. [Recruit](https://www.blackanchordesign.com/services/fort-worth-web-design/case-study) is a separate limited **$6,500** case-study offer for a five-page local-trades site. See the [pricing overview](../pricing/pricing-overview.md) and the [published pricing page](https://www.blackanchordesign.com/pricing) for scope and terms.

## Does Black Anchor use AI to build sites?
Sites and creative are designed and built by Black Anchor Design, not by AI. AI tools may be used to support research, ideation, and production efficiency. All final creative and strategic decisions are made by Bobby Robinson.
