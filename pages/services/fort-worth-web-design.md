Black Anchor Design builds websites for Texas contractors, infrastructure companies, manufacturers and specialty trades. The site organizes service information, comparable projects, qualifications and contact paths around the way owners, general contractors and facilities managers evaluate vendors.

## Contractor qualification checklist

Publish relevant completed projects, applicable credentials, an overview of safety practices, operating capabilities and the right business contacts. Include service territory, crew and equipment capacity, and insurance or bonding details where appropriate and approved for publication. The site supports an initial review. Formal prequalification follows the buyer’s or agency’s own requirements and submission process.
