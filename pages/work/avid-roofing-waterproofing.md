## The client
AVID Roofing & Waterproofing is a commercial roofing and waterproofing contractor in North Richland Hills, Texas.[^live-page] Its leadership and field supervision carry 205 combined years of experience, with more than $300M in work installed over the leadership's careers. Clients named on the page include Macy's, Pfizer, Wells Fargo, and Ben E. Keith.[^live-page]

## The brief
The site had to speak to facility directors, property managers, and general contractors, not homeowners. The goal was a website that reads like a prequalification package.[^live-page]

The positioning the site carries:
- Crews certified by Sika Sarnafil, Siplast, Garland, Johns Manville, and FiberTite
- A named project manager on every job who sends a daily report
- Honest advice on when coating or restoration makes sense and when it doesn't
- Roof systems: TPO, PVC, EPDM, modified bitumen, and standing seam metal[^live-page]

## What Black Anchor delivered
- **Brand work:** creative brief, brand psychology research, brand strategy, mood board direction, and brand guidelines[^live-page]
- **Website:** 59 pages covering 14 commercial services and 8 industries, with 48 industry FAQs
- **Service-area pages:** 34 in total, made up of 10 DFW county pages, 10 Tarrant County city pages, and 14 state pages
- **Case studies:** 4 waterproofing projects, including a 130,000 sq ft PVC reroof and a 56,000 sq ft acrylic coating job
- **Design process:** 3 homepage directions, each built as an 11-page site, before one was chosen
- **Print:** a duplex trifold with an emergency leak response agreement on the back, plus a fleet vehicle template for F-350 trucks[^live-page]

## Technical setup
- Duda build with Google Analytics, Search Console, and Tag Manager
- FAQPage schema on the industry pages
- 301 redirect map from the legacy site
- 100% uptime reported since the June 2026 launch[^live-page]

## Targets set for the site
- Organic traffic up 30% or more in the first six months
- Assessment inquiry conversion above 3% of targeted visitors
- Bounce rate below 40%
- Session duration above 2:30 on service and industry pages[^live-page]

These are goals stated on the page, not reported results.
