# Pricing Overview

## Website tiers
- **Commander:** $8,500–$10,500, up to 15 pages
- **Captain:** $11,000–$16,000, 16–25 pages
- **Vice Admiral:** $19,000–$27,000, 26–40 pages
- **Admiral:** from $36,000, 41–75 pages, 10–16 weeks
- **Fleet Admiral:** quoted, 76+ pages
- **Master Chief Diver (Shopify):** $17,500, up to 25 content pages — see [Shopify](../services/shopify-web-design.md)

## Ongoing
- Local SEO: $1,500/mo
- Boatswain: $1,940/mo
- Maintenance: $880/mo including 6 hours
- SEO plans: Quartermaster $1,500/mo, Boatswain $1,940/mo, Admiral's Watch $3,520/mo, $660 one-time setup

## Terms
- 15% discount: military / veterans / law enforcement / first responders only
- Pay-in-full discount capped at 5% across all tiers
- Book by December 31, 2026 for the remaining 2026 project slots; later bookings go into 2027.
