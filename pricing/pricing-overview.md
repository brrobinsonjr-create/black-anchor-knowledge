# Pricing Overview

Standard website packages start at **$8,500** with Commander.

## Website tiers
- **Commander:** $8,500–$10,500, up to 15 pages
- **Captain:** $11,000–$16,000, 16–25 pages
- **Vice Admiral:** $19,000–$27,000 for 26–40 pages, with a 10-week planning duration. Tailored 41–75-page scopes start at $36,000, with a 12–16-week planning duration.
- **Fleet Admiral:** quoted after discovery, typically 76+ pages or complex multi-division requirements
- **Master Chief Diver (Shopify):** $17,500, up to 25 content pages — see [Shopify](../services/shopify-web-design.md)

## Limited case-study offer
- **[Recruit](https://www.blackanchordesign.com/services/fort-worth-web-design/case-study):** $6,500 for a five-page local-trades site. The project becomes a case study. Offer terms and availability are listed on the linked page.

## Ongoing
- Local SEO: $1,500/mo
- Boatswain: $1,940/mo
- Maintenance: $880/mo including 6 hours
- SEO plans: Quartermaster $1,500/mo, Boatswain $1,940/mo, Admiral's Watch $3,520/mo, $660 one-time setup

## Terms
- 15% discount: military / veterans / law enforcement / first responders only
- Eligible new standard-package proposals receive 5% off for full payment at signing through December 31, 2026. Discounts cannot be combined. Recruit is ineligible.
- Book by December 31, 2026 for the remaining 2026 project slots; later bookings go into 2027.
